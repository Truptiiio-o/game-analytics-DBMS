## 📌 Project Overview
A free-to-play mobile game is 2 years old. Its player base is stable (~80k players across Jan–Apr 2026), but organic growth has plateaued and new players arrive almost exclusively through paid user acquisition. 

**The Studio's Goal:** Grow revenue without growing marketing spend.

**Key Telemetry Ground Rules:**
- **Revenue** strictly means real-money in-app purchases (`purchase.usd_amount > 0`).
- **Ad data** has no direct dollar revenue; `ad_view.reward_amount` represents virtual soft currency awarded to players (an in-game economy cost, not cash income).
- **Data Integrity:** Raw CSV files in `data/` are never modified on disk; all cleaning is executed through reproducible SQL views in DuckDB.

## ✂️ What I Dropped and Why (Triage & Prioritization)

In a real-world consulting or lab setting constrained by an **8–10 hour time budget**, prioritizing high-leverage business answers over academic over-engineering is essential. Here is what was dropped, along with the technical and business justification:

### 1. Dropped: Black-Box Machine Learning (XGBoost / Deep Neural Networks) for Churn
* **Why dropped:** The studio's Product Director needs an actionable decision rule, not an opaque probability score. Complex tree ensembles risk severe overfitting on short 120-day telemetry and require complex hyperparameter tuning.
* **What was done instead:** Built a clean, interpretable **Logistic Regression model** evaluated against a strong **Recency Baseline** ("inactive for $N$ days"). It delivered a **2.09&times; lift** in precision at the top 10% risk tier and directly exposed model coefficients that marketing managers can understand.

### 2. Dropped: 180+ Individual Country-Level Regression Controls
* **Why dropped:** Running granular fixed-effect regressions for 180 individual countries creates severe multi-collinearity, sparse data buckets for smaller countries, and uninterpretable coefficient tables for leadership.
* **What was done instead:** Used the studio's industry-standard **4-Tier Country Classification** (`Tier 1` through `Tier 4`) to run a rigorous **3-part shift-share decomposition** (within-tier effect, mix effect, interaction effect). This cleanly isolated 100% of the ARPDAU decline without statistical noise.

### 3. Dropped: Complex Parametric Lifetime Value (LTV) Curve Fitting (Pareto/NBD)
* **Why dropped:** Parametric models like Pareto/NBD or BG/NBD assume stable multi-year churn hazards and continuous Poisson purchase processes. Fitting these onto a truncated 120-day observation window introduces heavy parametric extrapolation error.
* **What was done instead:** Measured **empirical 30-day cumulative revenue per install** restricted strictly to cohorts with full 30-day maturity, accompanied by non-parametric **95% bootstrap confidence intervals**. This provided mathematically unassailable comparisons between channels.

### 4. Dropped: Ad Network eCPM / Virtual Economy Inflation Modeling
* **Why dropped:** The telemetry logs `ad_view.reward_amount` (soft virtual coins granted to players), but contains no external ad network mediation receipts (e.g., ironSource, AppLovin, or Unity Ads payout logs). Attempting to estimate ad monetization revenue would violate the core project rule: *"Never invent numbers."*
* **What was done instead:** Analyzed **ad cap saturation** (showing that Cap-5 players hit daily limits 20.8% of the time) and identified a major ad server tracking outage (March 5–7), providing actionable game balance recommendations to the monetization team.

### 5. Dropped: Heuristic Device De-duplication Beyond Canonical First-Seen Roots
* **Why dropped:** Complex probabilistic record linkage (matching players across IP addresses, session timestamps, or behavior patterns) carries high false-positive rates and risks contaminating player histories.
* **What was done instead:** Built a deterministic, idempotent mapping view (`device_map`) leveraging the explicit `original_device_id` telemetry field, collapsing 1,326 multi-device records into canonical root accounts without guessing.

## 🏆 Summary of Core Business Findings
1. **Monetisation Is Healthy:** Overall ARPDAU fell 16.3%, but ARPDAU within every country tier increased (+7.0% Tier 1, +8.5% Tier 2, +13.5% Tier 3, +1.9% Tier 4). The studio suffered from **Simpson's Paradox** driven by a 57-fold surge in low-monetising Tier 4 paid video installs.
2. **Whale Economy:** The top 1% of players generate **40.14%** of revenue; the top 10% generate **96.66%**. Over 70% of payers do not convert until Day 8 or later.
3. **Marketing Channel Confound:** `paid_social` is not inherently superior to `paid_video`. After controlling for country tier, the revenue difference is statistically indistinguishable from zero (95% CI $[-\$0.45, +\$0.21]$). Marketing budget should **not** be shifted based on naive numbers.
4. **Offer Economics:** $0.50 re-engagement offers can be broadly targeted, but $5.00 offers must be strictly reserved for historical spenders ($V \ge \$25$) to avoid net financial loss.
